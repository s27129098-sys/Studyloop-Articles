    About Studyloop Articles

    We're students too — which is exactly why we built this. Between classes and exam prep, half our reading time went into just finding something worth reading: good pieces on science, society, culture, and technology, scattered across a dozen sites, half of them behind a paywall and the other half not worth the click.

    So we decided to gather the useful ones in one place. Studyloop Articles is that place — pick a topic, read, and highlight whatever matters as you go. It's saved automatically, so you can pick up exactly where you left off.
